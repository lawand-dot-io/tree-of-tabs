The first release of Tree of Tabs: your tabs as a tree in Chrome's side panel.

- **Tabs a page opens nest under it**: links opened in a new tab, middle
  clicks and pages that open tabs. Tabs you open yourself start their own tree.
- **Drag and drop** to nest, reorder or move whole trees, also between windows.
  Chrome's tab strip always stays in the same order as the tree.
- **Collapse** trees you're not using: point at a parent and its icon turns
  into an arrow. Or turn collapsing off entirely.
- **Guide lines**, **tab group colors**, pinned tabs, and **split views** shown
  as one row.
- **Right-click actions**: new child tab, duplicate, pin, mute, indent and
  outdent, move to a new window, close a tree, close other tabs and more.
- **Keyboard** navigation, light and dark mode, and a tree that survives
  browser restarts.
- **Settings** for where new tabs go, what happens to a closed parent's
  children, density, indentation, full screen and more.

## Install

Until Tree of Tabs is on the Chrome Web Store: download
`tree-of-tabs-v1.0.0.zip` below and unzip it. Then open `chrome://extensions`,
turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder.
Needs Chrome 116 or newer.
